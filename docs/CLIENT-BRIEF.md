## Admin requirements
- Properties, packages, descriptions, photos and prices editable; add / delete / modify
- Calendars synced with the eZee (Yanolja) channel manager
- Discounts and packages; maintenance / live toggle per property
- WhatsApp message to VHI numbers after each booking (guest details, dates, discount, GST, property, guests)

## Analytics
Visitors, checkout reached, bookings, conversion; bookings by nights, guests, repeat guests, value; per-vertical and per-property stats; average nightly price, revenue, month-on-month growth.

## Guest experience
Book in 3–5 steps; offerings clear on the landing page; brand Vrindavan and the experience over accommodation; add-ons for pick & drop, scooty rental, reliable cab contacts; helpline number and email for guests who want to talk first. Meta ads will drive traffic, especially to Darshan Tours.
